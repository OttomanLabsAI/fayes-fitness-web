One place, not a list of links

This site does everything the Linktree did — and then some. Here’s the thinking.

The old way

The bio link opened a menu of doors, and every door led somewhere else — booking on one platform, brochures on another, signups on a third. Six destinations, six different looks, and none of them looked like Faye.

The new way

One home. Every offer has its own page, styled like Faye, doing the selling before any handoff happens.
