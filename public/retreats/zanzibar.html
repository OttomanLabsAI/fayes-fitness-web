Mind Fit Retreats are a safe space to fill your cup — workshops, classes and social activities that motivate you, help you reflect, let go and put things into perspective.

The Zanzibar edition happens at the Zen Boutique Resort, set in jungle a walk from the Indian Ocean. The first one — fitness, dancing, laughter, bonding and East African adventures — was the biggest retreat the resort had ever hosted, and the 2026 trip sold out. February 2027 is the next chance.
